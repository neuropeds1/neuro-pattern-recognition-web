Primary sources the draft is written against

Key Citation Used for
AHA/ASA-2023 Hoh BL, et al. 2023 Guideline for the Management of Patients With Aneurysmal Subarachnoid Hemorrhage. Stroke. 2023;54(7):e314–e370. Master reference: BP targets, timing of securing, nimodipine, seizure prophylaxis, DCI management, hyponatremia.
NCS-2011 Diringer MN, et al. Critical Care Management of Patients Following Aneurysmal SAH: Recommendations from the Neurocritical Care Society’s Multidisciplinary Consensus Conference. Neurocrit Care. 2011;15:211–240. Volume status, CSW/SIADH, fever, anemia/transfusion, hemodynamic augmentation.
NCS-MMM-2014 Le Roux P, et al. Consensus Summary Statement of the International Multidisciplinary Consensus Conference on Multimodality Monitoring. Neurocrit Care. 2014;21(S2). TCD thresholds, cEEG for DCI, PbtO2.
Vergouwen-2010 Vergouwen MDI, et al. Definition of DCI and cerebral infarction as outcome events. Stroke. 2010;41:2391–2395. DCI vs vasospasm definitions.
Connolly-2012 Connolly ES, et al. 2012 AHA/ASA SAH guideline. Stroke. 2012;43:1711–1737. Historical anchor for rebleeding epidemiology.
BRANT-1989 Pickard JD, et al. British aneurysm nimodipine trial. BMJ. 1989;298:636–642. Nimodipine outcome benefit.
ULTRA-2021 Post R, et al. Ultra-early tranexamic acid after SAH (ULTRA). Lancet. 2021;397:112–118. Antifibrinolytics not routinely recommended.
STASH-2014 Kirkpatrick PJ, et al. Simvastatin in aneurysmal SAH (STASH). Lancet Neurol. 2014. Statins not recommended for DCI prevention.
MASH-2 Mees SMD, et al. Magnesium for aneurysmal SAH (MASH-2). Lancet. 2012. IV magnesium not recommended.

Numbers flagged for exact-text check before sign-off

  • The precise SBP number to display pre-securing (guideline gives a range / soft ceiling, not one value).
  • DCI peak-day range (6–8 vs 7–10 across sources).
  • Shunt-dependence percentage to quote (published range ~8–48%).
  • Modified Fisher grade definitions and IVH handling.
  • Transfusion hemoglobin trigger during the DCI window.
  • Whether to state a nimodipine effect size at all, or just “improves outcome.”
  • Familial aneurysm screening criteria (number of affected relatives + risk factors).
